============================================================
1. OBJECTIVE
============================================================

To examine a suspicious email sample and identify
common phishing characteristics, including sender
spoofing, suspicious URLs, urgent language and
attempts to steal sensitive information.

============================================================
2. EMAIL DETAILS
============================================================

Sender:
security-alert@paypa1-support.example

Subject:
URGENT! Your PayPal Account Will Be Suspended
Within 24 Hours

Target URL:
http://paypa1-support.example/verify-account

============================================================
3. PHISHING INDICATORS IDENTIFIED
============================================================

1. SUSPICIOUS SENDER ADDRESS

The sender uses a lookalike domain containing
"paypa1" instead of "paypal".

2. DOMAIN IMPERSONATION

The sender domain does not belong to the official
PayPal domain.

3. URGENT LANGUAGE

The email demands immediate action and threatens
account suspension within 24 hours.

4. SUSPICIOUS URL

The provided URL uses a lookalike domain and
an HTTP connection instead of HTTPS.

5. CREDENTIAL THEFT ATTEMPT

The email requests account information and
password, which is a strong phishing indicator.

6. FEAR-BASED TACTICS

The message threatens permanent account blocking
to pressure the recipient into acting quickly.

7. GENERIC GREETING

The email starts with "Dear Customer" rather
than addressing the recipient by name.

============================================================

============================================================
6. FINDINGS
============================================================

Total Phishing Indicators Identified: 7

Major Findings:
- Lookalike sender domain
- Brand impersonation
- Suspicious verification link
- Credential harvesting attempt
- Urgency and fear-based manipulation

============================================================
7. CONCLUSION
============================================================

The analyzed email contains multiple characteristics
commonly associated with phishing attacks.

The sender impersonates a trusted payment service
and uses urgency, a suspicious URL and credential
requests to manipulate the recipient.

The email should be treated as suspicious and
should not be trusted.

============================================================
8. RECOMMENDATIONS
============================================================

1. Verify the sender's domain before responding.
2. Avoid clicking suspicious email links.
3. Never share passwords through email links.
4. Inspect email headers when available.
5. Verify suspicious requests through official
   websites or support channels.
6. Report suspected phishing emails.
